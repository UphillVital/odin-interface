# ODIN INTERFACE DESIGN DNA v1

## Direction

Style must express:
- AI
- advanced technology
- friendship between humans and technology
- everyday human-AI cooperation
- development of artificial models
- cosmic / alien control-room feeling
- calm clarity, not chaos

## Lesson Template Relationship

The existing lesson template may inspire:
- dark harmony
- readable blocks
- glow accents
- structured cards
- clean navigation
- Ukrainian/German multilingual sensitivity

But ODIN Interface must not become a lesson template.

## Design Principles

1. Clear hierarchy
2. No visual noise
3. Strong navigation
4. System status always visible
5. Buttons must describe real actions
6. Experimental zones separated from stable zones
7. Manual always accessible
